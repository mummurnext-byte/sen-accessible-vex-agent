Every robot program follows one visible pattern: input, Brain, output.

The Check button is our input. It tells the robot that something happened.

The Brain follows the Blocks from top to bottom. First, it displays PRESS CHECK. Then it waits for the button. When the input becomes true, the screen changes to GO.

Now watch the output. The drivetrain moves forward and stops at three hundred millimeters. When the movement finishes, the final output is DONE.

Your turn to predict. If we change three hundred to five hundred millimeters, the Brain still follows the same order, but the driving distance changes.
